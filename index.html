Totals in SOL at 0.1 SOL per trade; under each, trades won out of trades finished. Every bot buys at $2K–4K once a coin has enough early trades (Quality: 15+ trades by 10 seconds; Quantity: 10+ by 15 seconds), stops at a 60% loss and is out after 5 minutes.

Original method is the bot exactly as first built: it reads the formula market cap and assumes every sale is paid in full. That method is wrong on Mayhem coins. Real is the same rule on the real price, where a sale can never take out more SOL than the pot holds. Harsh pays you only from the SOL recorded in the pot; kind counts your own 0.1 SOL as still sitting in the pot. The truth is between those two.

Two newer bots run on real prices only (added about 20 minutes into the round). Half at 2x, rest to $15K buys like Quality, sells half once the price has doubled (that half returns the stake) and lets the rest ride to $15K or the 5-minute mark. Avoid rules, sell when pot falls buys like Quality but only when the pot already holds at least 0.1 SOL, has no price target, and sells when the pot has dropped 30% from its highest since the buy.
