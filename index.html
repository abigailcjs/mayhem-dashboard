Every bot buys 0.1 SOL, stops at a 60% loss and is out after 5 minutes. Prices and payouts are exact: the coin's true price from the chain after every trade, pump.fun's own formula, the 1.25% fee each way, and never more than the pot holds.

The sign is what coins that reached $9K had in common beforehand: pump.fun's bot has put in at least 0.2 SOL more than it has taken out, the pot holds 0.4 SOL or more, and the price is $2K–5K. The bots look for it from 15 seconds after launch until the coin is a minute old. In past coins the sign went on to $9K about one time in four, against one in fifteen for all coins. It was found by looking back, so this round is the test of whether it holds.
