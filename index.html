The three cards add up the five strictest bots (marked ★). They get about 3 trades an hour, so a fair read takes days. Each list puts the bot with the best total first. What "the sign" means is explained in the next section.

PERSON means the last trade before the bot buys was made by a human, not by pump.fun's own robot. If the robot made it, the coin is skipped. Three bots with only this rule started 20:06 UTC on 9 Nov.

★ Strictest (live since 20:28 UTC on 9 Nov) also need two more things. Bot quiet: pump.fun's robot has not traded for half a second. 6 people: at least 6 different people have already traded the coin (8 for the Quantity bot, which has no quiet rule).

In a replay of the 19 hours before, the strictest sign rule made 52 trades and won about 1 in 3. Those rules were found by looking back, so this is the test of whether they hold.

Two more bots went live 21:38 UTC on 9 Nov, each with the PERSON rule plus one new idea. Bought on a dip: the price has fallen to under 57% of the coin's highest price so far. Not frantic: fewer than 15 trades in the last 5 seconds. In the same replay the dip rule made 102 trades and won 29%; the not-frantic rule made 207 and won 25%. Both are hints, not proof.

Two more went live 02:21 UTC on 10 Nov: the dip rule, but selling half at $9K and half at $15K, once on its own and once also needing 6 people. They were the best of 224 mixes tried on the 5 hours before. Their replayed trades cover those same hours, so that part is bound to look good: they were picked because it did. Only their trades after 02:21 are a fair test.

Four more went live 03:48 UTC on 10 Nov: the same picky buying rules, but selling everything at $15K instead of $9K or in halves. They win less often and win bigger when they do. Looking back, this beat selling in halves over the 7 hours before, but lost to it on every rule in the 19 hours before that, so the replayed part proves nothing. Selling at $20K was also checked and was worse: too few coins get there inside the 5 minutes.

Every bot buys 0.1 SOL, stops at a 60% loss and is out after 5 minutes. Prices and payouts are exact: the coin's true price from the chain after every trade, pump.fun's own formula, the 1.25% fee each way, and never more than the pot holds.

The sign is what coins that reached $9K had in common beforehand: pump.fun's bot has put in at least 0.2 SOL more than it has taken out, the pot holds 0.4 SOL or more, and the price is $2K–5K. The bots look for it from 15 seconds (or 30 seconds) after launch until the coin is a minute old. In past coins the sign went on to $9K about one time in four, against one in fifteen for all coins. It was found by looking back, so this round is the test of whether it holds.

Looser sign needs only 0.1 SOL from the bot, 0.3 SOL in the pot and a price of $2K–4K. The two Old rule bots buy at $2K–4K after enough early trades (Quantity: 10+ by 15 seconds; Quality: 15+ by 10 seconds) with no sign, for comparison.
